Sahayak

Hostel Management & Dispatch System

Hostels run on broken plumbing, flickering lights, and administrative delays. When a student files a complaint through traditional paper logs, requests sit on desks while repairs stall for days. Sahayak changes that workflow entirely. Built to bridge communication between residents and hostel staff, it converts slow back-and-forth exchanges into direct, trackable action.

Instant Logging Students submit issues directly with specific room numbers and category tags.
Staff Queue Maintenance crews view a clean, prioritized list instead of scattered paper notes.
Live Updates Real-time status tracking keeps students informed without chasing wardens.
